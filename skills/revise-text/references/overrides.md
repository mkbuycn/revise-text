# Overrides

Your own rules. They apply after `humanize-writing/humanize-writing.md` and `humanize-writing/ai-tells.md`, and win
where they disagree. Updates to the bundled copy never touch this file.

When `/revise-text:insights` suggests a rule, add it here with the date and the finding it came from.

## Instructions and specs (tasks, READMEs, how-tos)

- Don't add opinions, first person, humor or "lived experience" asides (humanize-writing Pass 8). Instructions
  should sound like a competent colleague explaining a job, not a columnist.
- Never soften a limit or condition. "At most 200" stays "at most 200", never "around 200" or "up to roughly 200".
  "Only" and "must" stay where they are.
- Keep every path, command, field name and value exactly as written, in backticks.
- Bulleted lists are fine when the items really are separate steps or fields. Don't turn a list of required
  columns into prose.
- Don't add a closing summary, a "Good luck!" or a restatement of the goal.
